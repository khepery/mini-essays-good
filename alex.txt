主要投资银行作为关键中介，通过复杂的义务网络连接全球交易对手。当AIG对抵押贷款支持证券的敞口造成破产风险时，担心的不仅仅是一家公司——而是AIG为全球银行持有的数万亿资产提供了保险（BIS, 2023）。突然的AIG破产将迫使整个金融系统同时进行按市值计价的损失，可能引发银行倒闭的级联效应。中国2019年对包商银行的经验说明了受控干预：当局接管银行并保证存款，同时对银行间债权人施加损失，防止恐慌同时保持纪律。相互关联性证明了临时干预的合理性，以防止传染，这将对更广泛的经济造成远远超出初始失败的伤害。

医疗市场存在严重的信息不对称，患者无法评估治疗质量，而保险会产生逆向选择，病情较重的人不成比例地寻求保险。如果没有监管，保险公司通过挑选健康客户并拒绝为有既往病史的人提供保险来获利，从而造成低效率和不公平（WHO, 2023）。美国ACA之前的个人保险市场表明了这种失败，保险公司将资源花在医疗核保上而不是护理提供上。中国最近的医疗改革说明了有效监管：通过城乡保险计划强制基本覆盖，在2000年至2020年期间将覆盖率从30%大幅扩大到95%以上，改善了公平和公共卫生结果。覆盖和风险共担的监管授权纠正了这些市场失灵。

Price controls aim to protect vulnerable groups but consistently create unintended market distortions. I argue that binding price ceilings generate shortages and misallocation while price floors create wasteful surpluses, both reducing economic efficiency despite good intentions. **Body paragraph 1: Topic sentence 1 + facts/examples + mini conclusion  
正文1：主题句1 + 事实/例子 + 小结**

Price ceilings set below market equilibrium create excess demand that cannot be satisfied through normal market mechanisms, forcing alternative allocation systems. During the 1973-1974 oil embargo, U.S. gasoline price controls led to rationing through queuing: drivers waited hours in lines for fuel, creating massive time costs and inefficiency (OECD, 2024). Gas stations closed early when supplies ran out, and some states implemented odd-even rationing by license plate numbers. China's experience with pork price controls in 2019-2020 shows similar effects: when African swine fever reduced supply, price caps created shortages and black markets where unregulated sellers charged premium prices. The ceiling prevents prices from performing their rationing function, so shortages persist and alternative allocation mechanisms—queuing, favoritism, black markets—emerge, often benefiting well-connected insiders rather than the intended beneficiaries.

Inflation becomes entrenched when temporary price shocks trigger wage-price spirals and unanchored expectations. The 2021-2023 inflation surge demonstrates this pattern: pandemic-related supply chain disruptions and energy price spikes initially drove prices higher, but inflation persisted because workers demanded compensating wage increases and firms raised prices preemptively (OECD, 2024). China's experience controlling inflation in the mid-1990s illustrates the importance of credibility: the People's Bank of China's commitment to price stability helped reduce inflation from over 24% in 1994 to under 3% by 1997 without severe recession. Central banks combat inflation by raising benchmark interest rates, which transmits through multiple channels to reduce aggregate demand. **Body paragraph 1: Topic sentence 1 + facts/examples + mini conclusion

GDP measures market production value without considering distribution or whether spending enhances versus merely maintains welfare. A nation where income concentrates in the top 1% shows identical GDP to one with equal distribution, despite vastly different living standards for most citizens. Additionally, GDP counts defensive expenditures—pollution cleanup, crime prevention, disaster recovery—as positive contributions despite these merely offsetting harm (World Bank, 2024). China's rapid GDP growth from 2000-2020 illustrates this problem: economic output quintupled, but severe air pollution in major cities required massive healthcare and remediation spending that boosted GDP while reducing quality of life. GDP衡量市场生产价值，而不考虑分配或支出是增强还是仅仅维持福祉。收入集中在前1%的国家显示与收入平等分配的国家相同的GDP，尽管大多数公民的生活水平大不相同。此外，GDP将防御性支出——污染清理、犯罪预防、灾难恢复——计为积极贡献，尽管这些仅仅抵消了伤害（World Bank, 2024）。卡特里娜飓风的破坏和随后的重建都增加了美国GDP，但显然没有改善福祉。中国从2000年到2020年的快速GDP增长说明了这个问题：经济产出增长了五倍，但主要城市的严重空气污染需要大量医疗保健和补救支出，这些支出提高了GDP，同时降低了生活质量。此外，GDP忽略了无偿家务劳动：中国的老龄化人口严重依赖家庭老年护理，尽管提供了按市场价格价值数万亿的基本服务，但对GDP的贡献为零。
