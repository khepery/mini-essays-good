**T3 Tariffs, Globalization, and the Business Cycle**

**Prompt: Analyze how a new tariff affects GDP, inflation, and unemployment. Propose one policy response.**

**Introduction: Hook + Thesis + preview TS1 + preview TS2  
引言：钩子 + 论点 + 主题句1预览 + 主题句2预览**

A tariff can protect one factory while raising costs for many others. A new tariff usually raises domestic prices and reshapes output and employment through trade flows. I will explain its effects on GDP, inflation, and unemployment, then propose one policy response.  
关税可能保护一家工厂，却抬高许多行业的成本。 新关税通常会推高国内价格，并通过贸易流动重塑产出与就业。下面分析它对GDP、通胀和失业的影响，并提出一项政策应对。

**Body paragraph 1: Topic sentence 1 + facts/examples + mini conclusion  
正文1：主题句1 + 事实/例子 + 小结**

Tariffs increase the price of imported goods and inputs, so firms' costs rise and consumers face higher prices. Through uncertainty that delays investment decisions, real GDP can fall while inflation rises as costs pass through (IMF, 2024). Retaliation and uncertainty can further reduce investment and exports (World Bank, 2024).  
关税抬高进口商品与投入品价格，企业成本上升、消费者面临更高价格。通过不确定性导致投资推迟，实际GDP可能下降，而成本传导会推高通胀 (IMF, 2024)。报复与不确定性还会压低投资与出口 (World Bank, 2024)。

**Body paragraph 2: Topic sentence 2 + facts/examples + mini conclusion  
正文2：主题句2 + 事实/例子 + 小结**

Unemployment effects are uneven: protected producers may hire more, but downstream industries that use those inputs may cut jobs. A practical response is use fiscal transfers to offset consumer price increases instead of broad tariffs. This supports incomes and speeds adjustment without locking the economy into permanent protection.  
失业影响并不均衡：被保护行业可能增聘，但使用这些投入品的下游行业可能裁员。一个可行应对是用财政转移支付抵消物价上涨，而非广泛加税。这能稳定收入、加快调整，同时避免长期保护带来的效率损失。

**T4 Price Ceilings and Floors**  
**T4 最高限价与最低限价**

**Prompt: Explain the effects of a price ceiling or a price floor on consumers and producers.**  
**题目：解释最高限价或最低限价对消费者与生产者的影响。**

Suggested citations to practice: (National Statistics, 2024); (World Bank, 2023); (OECD, 2024)  
建议练习引用：(National Statistics, 2024); (World Bank, 2023); (OECD, 2024)

**Introduction: Hook + Thesis + preview TS1 + preview TS2  
引言：钩子 + 论点 + 主题句1预览 + 主题句2预览**

Good intentions do not cancel supply and demand. A price ceiling can help some consumers briefly but often creates shortages, while a price floor can support producers or workers but creates surplus or unemployment. I will explain both outcomes with examples.  
善意并不能取消供给与需求。 最高限价可能短期帮助部分消费者，但往往造成短缺；最低限价能支持生产者或劳动者，却会带来过剩或失业。下面用例子解释两者的结果。

**Body paragraph 1: Topic sentence 1 + facts/examples + mini conclusion  
正文1：主题句1 + 事实/例子 + 小结**

A ceiling below equilibrium raises quantity demanded and reduces quantity supplied, creating a shortage. In practice, food price ceilings that reduce quality and supply, leading to rationing, reduced quality, and sometimes informal markets (National Statistics, 2024). The benefit goes to those who still obtain the good, while others pay with time or no access.  
当最高限价低于均衡价时，需求增加、供给减少，结果是短缺。现实中，食品限价导致质量下降与供给减少，从而出现配给、质量下降，甚至非正规市场 (National Statistics, 2024)。能买到的人受益，其他人用排队时间或"买不到"来付出代价。

**Body paragraph 2: Topic sentence 2 + facts/examples + mini conclusion  
正文2：主题句2 + 事实/例子 + 小结**

A floor above equilibrium increases quantity supplied and reduces quantity demanded, so surplus appears. For example, a floor on taxi fares that raises driver income but reduces consumer surplus, which can require storage, buybacks, or reduced hiring (World Bank, 2023). Some groups gain, but society pays through higher prices, taxes, or unemployment.  
当最低限价高于均衡价时，供给超过需求，形成过剩。例如出租车底价提高司机收入但减少消费者剩余，可能需要收储、回购，或导致减少雇佣 (World Bank, 2023)。部分群体获益，但社会付出更高价格、税负或失业成本。

**Verification + final conclusion: limitation/counterpoint + final judgement  
结尾：验证/评价（局限或反方）+ 最终判断**

In emergencies, temporary ceilings may limit price gouging, which is an important limitation. Still, controls work best only when paired with supply expansion or targeted transfers; otherwise, hidden costs grow.  
在紧急情况下，临时限价能抑制哄抬价格，这是重要补充。但管制只有与增加供给或定向补贴配套才更有效，否则隐性成本会不断累积。

**T5 Inflation, CPI, and Interest Rates**  
**T5 通胀、CPI与利率**

**Prompt: Explain causes of inflation and how central banks respond using interest rates.**  
**题目：解释通胀原因以及央行如何用利率应对。**

Suggested citations to practice: (National Statistics, 2023); (BIS, 2023); (OECD, 2024)  
建议练习引用：(National Statistics, 2023); (BIS, 2023); (OECD, 2024)

**Introduction: Hook + Thesis + preview TS1 + preview TS2  
引言：钩子 + 论点 + 主题句1预览 + 主题句2预览**

Prices do not rise for one reason; inflation is a mix of forces. Inflation comes from demand pressure, supply shocks, and expectations. I will explain key causes and how central banks use interest rates to bring inflation down.  
价格上涨不是单一原因，通胀往往是多种力量叠加。 通胀来自需求压力、供给冲击与预期。下面解释主要成因，以及央行如何通过利率降低通胀。

**Body paragraph 1: Topic sentence 1 + facts/examples + mini conclusion  
正文1：主题句1 + 事实/例子 + 小结**

One major driver is currency depreciation that raises import prices. When spending or costs rise faster than output, firms raise prices, and expectations can make inflation persistent (National Statistics, 2023). If workers and firms expect inflation to continue, they set wages and prices higher today, reinforcing the cycle (BIS, 2023).  
一个重要驱动是货币贬值推高进口价格。当支出或成本上涨快于产出时，企业提价，而预期会让通胀更持久 (National Statistics, 2023)。如果公众预期通胀持续，工资与价格谈判就会更高，从而强化循环 (BIS, 2023)。

**Body paragraph 2: Topic sentence 2 + facts/examples + mini conclusion  
正文2：主题句2 + 事实/例子 + 小结**

Central banks respond by raising policy interest rates. Through a stronger currency lowers import-price inflation, though effects vary, credit and spending cool, so demand pressure falls and price growth slows. Because policy works with a lag, clear communication helps anchor expectations and reduce the required tightening.  
央行通常通过提高政策利率应对。通过更强货币降低输入型通胀，但效果因情境而异，信贷与支出降温，需求压力下降，价格涨幅放缓。由于政策有滞后，清晰沟通能锚定预期，减少需要的收紧幅度。

局限是利率上升可能在短期推高失业。因此央行往往循序渐进收紧，同时政府解决供给瓶颈；总体上利率仍是核心工具。

**T6 GDP and Standard of Living**  
**T6 GDP与生活水平**

**Prompt: Explain why GDP may not fully measure standard of living and suggest one better indicator.**  
**题目：解释为什么GDP不能完全衡量生活水平，并提出一个更好的指标。**

Suggested citations to practice: (UNEP, 2023); (ILO, 2024); (World Bank, 2024)  
建议练习引用：(UNEP, 2023); (ILO, 2024); (World Bank, 2024)

**Introduction: Hook + Thesis + preview TS1 + preview TS2  
引言：钩子 + 论点 + 主题句1预览 + 主题句2预览**

GDP counts output, not wellbeing. GDP is important for tracking output, but it does not fully measure living standards. I will explain two limitations and then suggest one better indicator.  
GDP统计产出，却不直接统计福祉。 GDP用于追踪产出很重要，但不能完全衡量生活水平。下面说明两点局限，再提出一个更好的指标。

**Body paragraph 1: Topic sentence 1 + facts/examples + mini conclusion  
正文1：主题句1 + 事实/例子 + 小结**

GDP counts market activity, not wellbeing. For example, it overlooks quality improvements and leisure time, and it misses unpaid work like caregiving and volunteering, so policymakers may miss real problems (UNEP, 2023). A country can grow while health, environment, or security worsens.  
GDP统计市场活动，却不等同于福祉。例如它忽视质量提升与闲暇时间，以及它遗漏照护与志愿服务等无偿劳动，这会让政策忽视真实问题 (UNEP, 2023)。一个国家可能增长，但健康、环境或安全在变差。

**Body paragraph 2: Topic sentence 2 + facts/examples + mini conclusion  
正文2：主题句2 + 事实/例子 + 小结**

A better indicator is Human Development Index (HDI) to include health and education. It adds health, education, environment, or typical income, which links more directly to how people live (ILO, 2024). In practice, using a small dashboard of indicators alongside GDP gives a clearer picture.  
一个更好的指标是用人类发展指数（HDI）纳入健康与教育。它加入健康、教育、环境或典型收入等维度，更直接对应人们的真实生活 (ILO, 2024)。实践中，把这些指标与GDP一起做成小型"仪表盘"，会更清晰。

**Verification + final conclusion: limitation/counterpoint + final judgement  
结尾：验证/评价（局限或反方）+ 最终判断**

The limitation is that wellbeing indices require judgement about weights. Even so, the best approach is to keep GDP for output and use broader indicators to judge living standards.  
局限在于福祉指标需要设定权重，带有判断性。尽管如此，最佳做法仍是：GDP用于产出，生活水平用更全面的指标评估。
